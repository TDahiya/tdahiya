For my MSc practicum I built a 1,645-prompt adversarial benchmark across 5 production LLMs, proposed a novel **IIR metric** for measuring safety consistency, and found **60.2% strict inconsistency** across frontier models — proving safety alignment and behavioural consistency are independent properties.

## 🔬 Research

**LLM Safety Consistency Under Adversarial Paraphrasing** · MSc Practicum, DCU 2025–2026  
→ [github.com/TDahiya/llm-safety-adversarial](https://github.com/TDahiya/llm-safety-adversarial)

- Designed 329 adversarial seed prompts grounded in 14 MITRE ATT&CK techniques, expanded to **1,645 variants** using 4 paraphrasing strategies
- Built a multi-agent pipeline querying 5 production LLMs (Claude, GPT-4o, DeepSeek, Mistral, Qwen) — **8,225 responses**, **24,675 G-EVAL verdicts**
- Proposed novel **IIR (Intent-level Inconsistency Rate)** metric — found **60.2% strict inconsistency** across models
